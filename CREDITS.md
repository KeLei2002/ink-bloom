# Ink Bloom credits

## Game direction

The designer chose the ink drop and four-scroll format, set the keyboard and mouse controls, and used feedback from two players to revise the action hints and final stretch of each painting.

## Pictures and sound

Cursor’s image generator made the four paintings, the worm-eaten paper, the characters and the incense burner for this game. They draw on Chinese ink-wash and blue-green landscape painting. The original files are in `art/raw/`. Cursor assisted with the game and its original music and sound effects. Codex helped revise the game after playtesting. The soundtrack is generated in the game. The cast is entirely drawn.
