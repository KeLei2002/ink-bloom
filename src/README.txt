墨染 Ink Bloom

怎么玩：双击 index.html，用桌面浏览器（Chrome、Edge、Safari、Firefox）打开，点「开始」。
How to play: double-click index.html, open it in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox)
and click Start.

操作只有四样 / The only controls
  W A S D            移动 / move
  鼠标 / mouse        瞄准 / aim
  按住鼠标左键         泼墨 / hold the left button to spray ink
  点鼠标右键           朝鼠标方向冲刺 / right-click to dash toward the mouse
菜单、重画、声音、语言都是画面上的按钮。
Menu, restart, sound and language are buttons on the screen.

目标：修复四幅古画。前三幅染回 80% 即可过关。染到 60% 后，往青圈内泼墨或从圈心冲过去，就能晕开一大片。最后一幅要赶走蠹王。
右键冲刺开场就能用。底栏给出操作提示；破壳后「泼它」跟着大蠹虫，被咬扣掉的秒数在顶栏香条旁显示。
第一幅不会输。从第二幅起有一炷香，香烧完之前没染够就要重画这一幅（只重画这一幅）。
最后一幅是蠹王：只有尾巴怕墨。输了两次，游戏会提出降低难度；「闲笔」一档不限时。
Goal: restore 80% of each of the first three paintings and drive off the King in the fourth.
At 60%, spray inside the jade ring or dash through it to bloom a broad patch of ink.
Dash is ready from the start. Read action hints below the painting. “Spray it!” follows a cracked
grub and lost seconds appear beside the incense timer. Ink only reaches a ring around the drop,
so keep moving while you spray. The first painting cannot be lost. From the second on, a stick of
incense burns: fall short when it burns out and that painting (only that one) starts again. The
last one is the Bookworm King: only its tail takes ink. After two losses the game offers a lower
difficulty; the "Gentle" row has no time limit.
